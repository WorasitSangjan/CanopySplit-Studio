CanopySplit Studio

1. Select a dataset

Folder structure

Put JPG, JPEG, or PNG photos directly in one folder.

photos/
  day1_RGB_group1.jpg
  day1_NIR_group1.jpg  (optional)
  day2_RGB_group1.jpg

Filename format

  • day1 is day 1 of the experiment, as shown in the results.
  • group1 identifies plants photographed together. Keep the same group ID across days.
  • RGB is the required colour photo. Use one per group and day.
  • NIR (near-infrared) is optional. Match the RGB photo’s day and group ID for relative NDVI.

2. Run measurement

    Physical area will be reported in cm².

    Measurements and statistics

    • Leaf area. Pixels, physical area, and fraction of pot area.
    • Colour and greenness. Relative RGB, VARI, GLI, NGRDI, and ExG.
    • Leaf damage. Damaged area and damage by colour from RGB photos.
    • Near-infrared. Relative NIR and NDVI from usable RGB/NIR pairs.

    Choose a measurement to customize its statistics. Other measurements use the defaults.

    Percentile values can range from 0 to 100. Enter up to three.
